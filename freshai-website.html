      Smart storage

      Storage recommendations by category

      General guidance FreshAI applies automatically based on what's detected.

      🌤️

      Room Temperature

      Bananas, tomatoes, onions, potatoes, whole melons. Keep in a cool, dry, ventilated spot away from direct sun. 18–22°C · 50–60% humidity.

      ❄️

      Refrigerated

      Leafy greens, dairy, cooked leftovers, most fruit. Store in crisper drawers to manage humidity. 1–4°C · 85–95% humidity for produce.

      🧊

      Frozen

      Raw meat, seafood, and bread you won't use in 3 days. Wrap tightly to prevent freezer burn. -18°C or below.

        About FreshAI

        Built to keep food out of the bin

        FreshAI combines computer vision with nutrition science to help households and small kitchens cut food waste, eat safer, and plan meals around what's actually in the fridge — not what's on a shopping list.

        🎯

        Our mission

        Roughly a third of food produced globally is wasted. We want scanning your fridge to feel as normal as checking the weather.

        🧪

        The model

        A convolutional network fine-tuned on labeled freshness datasets across produce, dairy, meat and seafood categories.

        🔐

        Privacy first

        Photos are processed for analysis and not sold or shared. You can delete your scan history anytime.

        🌱

        Impact

        Every avoided spoil is fewer emissions from landfill methane and wasted farm resources.
